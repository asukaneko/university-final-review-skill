# Natural Sciences / Mathematics

Use this guide for mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, environmental science, statistics, and laboratory-based science courses.

## Review focus

- Core concepts, laws, theorems, definitions, and assumptions
- Formula derivations and valid application conditions
- Experimental methods, variables, controls, errors, and interpretation
- Graphs, diagrams, reaction processes, models, and data tables
- Proofs, calculations, and scientific reasoning chains

## Recommended chapter structure

1. Concept map and key terms
2. Laws, theorems, formulas, or biological mechanisms
3. Assumptions and application conditions
4. Step-by-step derivations or process explanations
5. Experiment or data interpretation templates
6. Worked calculation / proof examples
7. Common misconceptions
8. Practice questions and answer explanations

## Typical question types

- Definition and concept identification
- Formula selection and calculation
- Derivation or proof
- Diagram labeling
- Experimental design and error analysis
- Data interpretation
- Mechanism explanation

## Output emphasis

Always clarify units, assumptions, boundary conditions, and the reasoning path from known information to conclusion.
