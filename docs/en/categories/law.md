# Law

Use this guide for constitutional law, civil law, criminal law, administrative law, commercial law, international law, procedural law, jurisprudence, and other legal courses.

## Review focus

- Legal concepts, doctrines, elements, principles, and statutory structures
- Rule definitions and conditions of application
- Case facts, holdings, reasoning, and legal significance
- Issue spotting and structured legal analysis
- Comparison between similar legal concepts or institutions

## Recommended chapter structure

1. Core legal concepts and definitions
2. Legal rules, elements, and exceptions
3. Statutory or doctrinal framework
4. Case summary and reasoning when applicable
5. Issue-rule-application-conclusion templates
6. Concept comparison table
7. Common exam traps
8. Practice hypotheticals with model answers

## Typical question types

- Definition and doctrine explanation
- Statutory element identification
- Case brief questions
- Legal issue spotting
- Hypothetical case analysis
- Compare-and-contrast questions
- Essay questions on legal principles

## Output emphasis

Use structured legal reasoning. For case-analysis questions, prefer IRAC or a similar issue-rule-application-conclusion structure.

## Safety note

Outputs are for legal study and exam preparation only. Do not present generated content as legal advice.
