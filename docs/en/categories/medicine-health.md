# Medicine / Health

Use this guide for medicine, nursing, pharmacy, public health, anatomy, physiology, pathology, microbiology, clinical medicine, and health-related courses.

## Review focus

- Anatomical structures, physiological mechanisms, pathological processes, and pharmacological principles
- Disease definition, etiology, pathogenesis, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, treatment principles, and prevention
- Clinical reasoning chains and differential diagnosis patterns
- Safety-critical details, contraindications, adverse reactions, dosage concepts, and nursing precautions
- Public health frameworks, epidemiological indicators, prevention strategies, and health policy concepts

## Recommended chapter structure

1. Core terminology and normal structure / function
2. Mechanism or disease process
3. Key signs, symptoms, tests, indicators, and risk factors
4. Diagnosis or classification logic when applicable
5. Treatment, prevention, nursing, or public-health intervention principles
6. Clinical case reasoning template
7. High-risk mistakes and safety cautions
8. Practice questions with explanations

## Typical question types

- Definition and terminology questions
- Structure-function matching
- Mechanism explanation
- Case analysis
- Differential diagnosis or clinical reasoning
- Drug mechanism / adverse reaction / contraindication questions
- Public-health indicator calculation or interpretation

## Safety note

Outputs must be framed as study support for exams, not medical advice. Do not present generated content as a substitute for professional diagnosis, treatment, or clinical decision-making.
