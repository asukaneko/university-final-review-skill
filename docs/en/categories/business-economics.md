# Business / Economics

Use this guide for economics, finance, accounting, marketing, management, operations, entrepreneurship, business analytics, strategy, and organizational behavior.

## Review goal

Turn business and economics materials into frameworks for model selection, calculation, case diagnosis, evidence-based recommendation, and managerial judgment under constraints.

## What to extract from materials

- Models, frameworks, definitions, assumptions, diagrams, formulas, and decision rules.
- Financial statements, ratios, accounting rules, valuation methods, cost behavior, and budget logic.
- Economic graphs, equilibrium analysis, elasticity, optimization, welfare, and policy effects.
- Marketing/strategy/management cases, frameworks, stakeholder maps, and implementation risks.
- Quantitative methods, data tables, dashboards, regression outputs, and interpretation rules.
- Past case questions, calculation formats, and marking rubrics.

## Chapter note structure

1. **Decision problem**: what business/economic decision the chapter supports.
2. **Core framework/model**: assumptions, variables, diagram, when to use.
3. **Formula and calculation sheet**: symbols, steps, interpretation.
4. **Case application guide**: facts to extract, diagnosis, options, recommendation.
5. **Graph/table interpretation**: axes, shifts, trends, managerial meaning.
6. **Comparison table**: models, strategies, financial ratios, market structures.
7. **Worked examples**: numeric, graph-based, and case-based.
8. **Risk and limitation notes**.
9. **Common mistakes**: confusing profit/cash, correlation/causation, average/marginal, strategy/tactic.
10. **Practice set**.

## Typical exam question types

- Define concepts and compare frameworks.
- Calculate ratios, costs, NPV, elasticity, equilibrium, forecasts, or accounting entries.
- Interpret graphs, tables, financial statements, regression output, or dashboards.
- Analyze business cases and recommend action.
- Evaluate strategy, market behavior, policy effect, or organizational problem.
- Write concise memos or executive recommendations.

## Analysis templates

### Quantitative business/economics problem

1. Identify the decision or model.
2. List data, assumptions, time period, and units/currency.
3. Apply formula step by step.
4. Interpret result: so what for the decision?
5. Test sensitivity or limitation.
6. State recommendation if required.

### Case analysis

Use: **Situation → Problem → Evidence → Framework → Options → Evaluation criteria → Recommendation → Implementation risks**.

### Economics graph question

Use: **Initial equilibrium → Shock/change → Curve movement → New equilibrium → Welfare/distribution effect → Policy implication**.

## Question bank rules

Generate:

- 20% concept/framework recall.
- 25% calculation and graph/table interpretation.
- 30% case diagnosis and recommendation.
- 15% essay/policy/strategy evaluation.
- 10% integrated mixed-format questions.

Answers should include interpretation and managerial/economic significance, not just numbers.

## High-yield exam prediction signals

Prioritize topics that:

- Combine formulas/graphs with real decisions.
- Are repeated in assignments, case discussions, and tutorials.
- Have standard frameworks such as SWOT, Porter, STP, 4Ps, NPV, break-even, supply-demand.
- Involve common calculation formats or decision trade-offs.

## Active study plan

- Build a model-selection table: when to use each framework/formula.
- Drill calculations until setup becomes automatic.
- Practice cases with timed one-page recommendations.
- Use active recall for diagrams and formula interpretation.
- Compare similar metrics and frameworks to avoid substitution errors.

## Scoring rubric

Award marks for model/framework choice, correct calculation, interpretation, evidence use, recommendation quality, risk awareness, and concise business communication.
