# Business / Economics

Use this guide for economics, management, marketing, accounting, finance, organizational behavior, operations management, entrepreneurship, and related business courses.

## Review focus

- Core concepts, models, assumptions, indicators, and decision frameworks
- Graphs, formulas, accounting entries, financial statements, and quantitative tools
- Market mechanisms, firm behavior, strategy, organizational processes, and policy effects
- Case analysis and management decision reasoning
- Calculation procedures and interpretation of results

## Recommended chapter structure

1. Concept and model overview
2. Key formulas, graphs, indicators, or frameworks
3. Assumptions and application conditions
4. Step-by-step calculation or case-analysis template
5. Interpretation of results
6. Common exam traps
7. Practice calculations, short answers, and cases
8. Mock exam rubric

## Typical question types

- Concept explanation
- Graph interpretation
- Calculation and financial analysis
- Case analysis
- Strategy or management decision questions
- Compare-and-contrast questions
- Essay or policy analysis

## Output emphasis

Connect concepts to decisions. For quantitative questions, show formulas, steps, units, and interpretation. For case questions, provide structured analysis and feasible recommendations.

## Safety note

Outputs are for study and exam preparation only, not financial, investment, accounting, or business advice.
